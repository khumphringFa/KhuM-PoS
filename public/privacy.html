Privacy and data use

Effective 15 November 2026 · KhuM PoS v0.1

KhuM PoS is a point-of-sale platform operated by Manas Tripura. This page explains the app's current storage and account access.

Information the app uses

Google sign-in provides your Google account identifier, name and verified email address. KhuM PoS uses these to identify your account and assign shop permissions. The app does not receive your Google password.

Shop records include shop name, address and phone, product details, prices, costs, stock quantities, receipts, discounts, recorded payment methods and team membership. Payment methods are recorded for bookkeeping; this version does not process bank payments or store card numbers.

Local and cloud storage

The local workspace is stored in your device's app/browser storage. When you use a cloud shop, its records synchronize to the platform owner's Cloudflare D1 database. Cloudflare hosts the application and processes requests. Offline sales stay on the device until successful synchronization; conflicting uploads remain pending for review.

Google login does not automatically copy shop data to Google Drive. Automated Google Drive backups are not enabled in this version. Exported backup and receipt files are stored or shared only through the actions you initiate.

Who can access shop records

Shop owners and their invited cashiers can access their assigned shop according to their roles. Other shopkeepers cannot access that shop through the app. The platform's Master Super Admin can access and manage all centrally stored shop records, including stock, prices and sales. This is disclosed when a cloud shop is created. Administrator access and selected management actions are logged.

Accounts, devices and retention

Account sessions expire and can be signed out. Signing out preserves cached workspace records on that device for later use. Removing a cashier or suspending a shop blocks future API access but cannot erase records already cached offline. Protect your phone and browser profile and export pending sales before uninstalling or clearing storage.

Cloud business records remain stored until the platform operator removes them or applicable retention needs are addressed. There is currently no self-service account or shop deletion screen. Contact the operator to request access, correction or deletion, and identify the account and shop involved without sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive data.
